# The Starter Stays App-Agnostic In v1.0

The Starter targets full-stack developers and small teams who bring their own application-stack choices, so v1.0 deliberately omits auth, database, payments, i18n, analytics, CMS, and email. Bundling any of those would force one opinion on every App and grow a maintenance surface the Starter cannot carry. The value offered is the runtime, rendering model, and tooling rather than an application framework; revisit this only if the product pivots toward being one.
